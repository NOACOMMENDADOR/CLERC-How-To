Device Security & Employee Separation

How to handle lost or stolen laptops, employee departures, and remote device actions through Microsoft Intune.

Purpose

This SOP covers what to do when a CLERC laptop is lost or stolen, or when an employee leaves the organization. It explains the remote actions available through Microsoft Intune, how BitLocker encryption protects data on missing devices, and the steps for disabling an employee's Microsoft account.

These procedures apply whether the separation is voluntary, involuntary, or the result of a missing device.

Microsoft Intune Windows 11 BitLocker Microsoft 365 Admin

Key Concepts

Remote Wipe vs. Retire

Primary action

Wipe (Factory Reset)

Erases everything on the device and restores it to factory settings. The device is removed from Intune management.

Use when: You want to completely clean the device, whether for reassignment or because it is lost/stolen. This is the more common action at CLERC.

Rarely used

Retire (Remove Management)

Removes only the Intune management profile, company apps, and company policies. Personal files and apps are left intact.

Use when: An employee was using a personal device enrolled in Intune and you need to remove company data without erasing their personal files. CLERC does not currently have personal devices enrolled.

⚠ Both actions require the device to be powered on and connected to the internet to take effect. If a stolen laptop is powered off, the wipe command will execute the next time it connects.

🔒 BitLocker Protection

All CLERC workstations are required to have BitLocker encryption enabled (per the compliance policy). BitLocker encrypts the entire hard drive. Even if a laptop is stolen, the data on the drive cannot be read without either the user's Windows login or the BitLocker recovery key. A thief who pulls the hard drive out of a stolen laptop cannot read any files on it.

BitLocker recovery keys for enrolled devices are stored in Intune (see the Compliance Policy SOP, Section 6). After a device is wiped remotely, its recovery key is no longer needed since all data has been erased.

Section 1 Lost or Stolen Device

⚡

Speed matters. The remote wipe only works when the device connects to the internet. Take these steps immediately when an employee reports a laptop as lost or stolen.

Step 1

Remote wipe the device

Wipe the device from Intune (see Section 4 for detailed steps). This queues a factory reset that will execute the next time the device connects to the internet.

Step 2

Disable or reset the Microsoft account

If the laptop was stolen and there is any chance the thief could guess the password: disable the employee's Microsoft account entirely (see Section 5).

If the laptop was lost (left in a restaurant, fell out of a bag): disabling the account may not be necessary if the employee has a strong password, but use your judgment.

Alternative: Reset the employee's Microsoft password instead of fully disabling the account. This blocks sign-in on the stolen device without disrupting the employee's access from other devices once they set a new password.

Step 3

File a police report (if stolen)

Record the device serial number (found in Intune under the device's hardware details) for the report.

Step 4

Document the incident

Note the date, circumstances, which device was affected, and what actions you took.

What Happens Next

If the device connects to the internet: The wipe command executes automatically. The device is factory reset and removed from Intune. No further action is needed on that device.

If the device never connects: The wipe command stays queued in Intune indefinitely but never executes. The data on the device remains protected by BitLocker encryption. Without the Windows password or recovery key, the data is inaccessible. You can delete the device record from Intune after a reasonable period (30 days or so) to keep your device list clean.

Section 2 Planned Employee Departure

When an employee gives notice or a termination is planned in advance, retrieve the device before the employee's last day.

Step 1

Retrieve the laptop

Collect the laptop on or before the employee's last day.

Step 2

Back up needed files

Check the Desktop, Documents, and Downloads folders. Copy anything the employee or CLERC needs to keep to a USB drive or shared storage.

Step 3

Wipe the device

Wipe the device from Intune (Section 4) or wipe it locally using the standard laptop offboarding procedure. Either method works since you have the device in hand.

Step 4

Disable the Microsoft account

Disable or delete the employee's Microsoft account through the Microsoft 365 admin center (Section 5). Disabling is preferred over deleting so you retain access to their mailbox and OneDrive files for a transition period.

Step 5

Set up the device for the next employee

Use the standard laptop setup procedure. When the new employee signs in with their CLERC Microsoft account, the device will auto-enroll in Intune and receive all apps and policies.

ⓘ Disable the Microsoft account on the employee's last day, not before. They need their email and account access through their final day of work.

Section 3 Employee Refuses to Return Device

If a terminated employee does not return their CLERC laptop, you can still protect company data remotely.

Step 1

Remote wipe the device

Wipe the device from Intune (Section 4). This queues a full factory reset.

Step 2

Disable the Microsoft account immediately

This prevents the employee from signing in to Microsoft 365 services (email, OneDrive, Teams) from any device. See Section 5.

Step 3

Reset the Microsoft password

As an additional precaution, reset the password. Even if the employee is signed in on the device, their cached credentials will expire and they will be unable to re-authenticate.

Step 4

Document everything

Record the date of termination, the device serial number and name from Intune, the date you issued the remote wipe, and any communication with the employee about returning the device.

Step 5

Follow up on device return

Work with CLERC leadership on next steps for retrieving the physical device. The remote wipe protects the data, but the hardware still belongs to CLERC.

What Protects CLERC's Data

Even if the employee keeps the laptop and avoids connecting it to the internet (preventing the remote wipe), CLERC's data is protected by multiple layers:

🔒 BitLocker encryption The drive is encrypted. If they reinstall Windows to bypass the login screen, BitLocker will prompt for a recovery key they do not have, and the encrypted data from the previous installation is unreadable.
🚫 Disabled account They cannot access CLERC email, OneDrive, SharePoint, or Teams from any device.
🔐 Password reset Their cached sign-in on the laptop will expire. They will be prompted to re-authenticate and will be unable to do so.
☁ No offline cloud access Files stored in OneDrive or SharePoint are not fully available offline unless the employee specifically synced them before separation.
⚠ The main risk is files saved locally on the laptop's hard drive (not in OneDrive). BitLocker protects these as long as the employee cannot sign in. If you have reason to believe sensitive local files exist on the device, escalate to CLERC leadership promptly.

Section 4 How to Remote Wipe a Device

Step 1

Sign in to the Intune portal

Open intune.microsoft.com and sign in with your admin account.

Step 2

Navigate to the device

Devices ➜ All devices ➜ [select the device]
Step 3

Click Wipe

Click Wipe in the top toolbar.

Step 4

Configure wipe options

A confirmation dialog will appear:

  • Leave Wipe device, but keep enrollment state and associated user account unchecked (you want a full wipe).
  • Optionally check Wipe device even if device loses power. This attempts to continue the wipe if the device loses power mid-process. Recommended for lost/stolen devices.
Step 5

Confirm the wipe

Click Wipe to confirm. The wipe status will show as Pending in Intune and will change to Completed once the device connects and the wipe executes.

If You Need to Retire Instead

To remove only Intune management (keeping personal files intact):

  1. Follow steps 1 through 2 above.
  2. Click Retire instead of Wipe.
  3. Confirm the action.

This removes company apps, policies, and the management profile. The device is no longer managed by Intune. Use this only for personal devices that were enrolled in Intune.

Section 5 Disabling an Employee's Microsoft Account

Disabling (blocking sign-in) is preferred over deleting the account. A disabled account preserves the employee's mailbox and OneDrive files for a transition period. You can delete the account later once all data has been transferred.

Block Sign-In

  1. Sign in to admin.microsoft.com (Microsoft 365 admin center).
  2. Go to:
    Users ➜ Active users ➜ [select employee]
  3. Click Block sign-in (or find it under the account's options menu).
  4. Check Block this user from signing in and click Save changes.

The employee will be signed out of all Microsoft 365 services within 60 minutes. Cached sign-ins on devices will stop working as tokens expire.

Reset Password (Additional Precaution)

Resetting the password forces re-authentication immediately on the next sign-in attempt, rather than waiting for token expiration.

On the same user page in the admin center, click Reset password. Generate a random password. The employee will be unable to sign in with their old password.

Reassign or Delete the Account Later

After blocking sign-in, the account and its data (mailbox, OneDrive files) remain available for admin access. When you are ready:

To access their files

In the Microsoft 365 admin center, you can grant another user access to the former employee's OneDrive and mailbox through the account deletion wizard, or by adding yourself as a delegate.

To free up the license

Delete the account when you no longer need access to their data. Microsoft retains deleted account data for 30 days before permanent deletion. Go to Users > Deleted users if you need to restore an account within that window.

⚠ If CLERC has a limited number of Microsoft 365 licenses, you may need to delete the account (or remove its license) promptly to free the license for a new employee. Back up or transfer any needed files first.
