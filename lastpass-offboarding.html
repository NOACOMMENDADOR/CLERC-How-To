Laptop Offboarding & Reset

Standard operating procedure for returning a CLERC-owned Windows laptop to a clean state before reassignment.

Purpose

Ensure company information is retained when required, employee data is not unintentionally lost, and a Windows laptop is returned to a clean state before reassignment to another employee.

Windows 11 Google Drive Airtable

Scope

This SOP applies to CLERC-owned Windows laptops that are being returned by an employee and prepared for internal reuse. Account deactivation, license removal, Intune actions, and other identity-management tasks may be covered by separate procedures.

⚠ Disposal or Transfer Outside CLERC

The standard reset in this SOP is intended for internal reassignment. If a device will be sold, donated, recycled, returned to a vendor, or otherwise leave CLERC control, follow an approved secure-erasure/decommissioning process rather than relying on this internal-reuse reset alone.

Required Access

Admin Account CLERC Microsoft administrator account (admin@clerc.co) or an approved local Administrator account
Cloud Storage Access Access to the employee's CLERC Google Drive or other authorized storage location if file verification is required
💡
Have the power adapter and a reliable internet connection available. The cloud download reset requires both.

Procedure Offboarding Steps

STEP 1

Confirm the Device Is Ready for Offboarding

Verify the laptop has been returned with its charger and any assigned accessories. Record the asset tag and serial number before making changes, and confirm the device matches the employee assignment in Airtable.

STEP 2

Verify Business Files Are Retained

Employees should store working files in CLERC's approved cloud storage. Before wiping the laptop, check common local storage locations for business files that may not have been synced:

  • Check Desktop, Downloads, and Documents.
  • Check any known project-specific local folders.
  • Confirm files that must be retained are copied or synced to the appropriate CLERC Google Drive location.
⚠
Do not copy personal files unless there is a documented business or records-retention reason to do so.
STEP 3

Confirm Backup Is Complete

Do not continue until required company files have been verified in their destination.

◼
Stop point: If important files are found locally and ownership or retention is unclear, stop and ask the employee's supervisor or the appropriate records owner before wiping the device.
STEP 4

Sign In with an Administrator Account

Sign in using the CLERC Microsoft administrator account (admin@clerc.co) or an approved local Administrator account.

🔑
Do not use the departing employee's credentials unless specifically required and authorized.
STEP 5

Start Windows Reset

Open Settings > System > Recovery. Under Recovery options, select Reset PC.

STEP 6

Choose the Reset Options

  1. Select Remove everything.
  2. Select Cloud download.
  3. Review the summary before starting and confirm that personal files, apps, and settings will be removed.
  4. Start the reset.
💡
"Remove everything" + "Cloud download" gives the cleanest result. The cloud download reinstalls Windows from Microsoft's servers rather than from the local recovery image.
STEP 7

Allow the Reset to Finish

Keep the laptop connected to power and internet. The process commonly takes about an hour, but actual time varies by device and connection speed.

⚠
Do not interrupt power during the reset. A power loss mid-reset can leave the device in an unrecoverable state.
STEP 8

Stop at Windows First-Run Setup

When the laptop restarts to the Windows out-of-box experience (the Region screen), do not create a new user. The laptop is now ready for the onboarding procedure.

✅
The device should be sitting at the Region selection screen. This is the starting point for the Windows Laptop Setup Guide.
STEP 9

Update Airtable and Device Records

Update the relevant Airtable employee and device/equipment records:

  • Remove or update the employee-to-device assignment as applicable.
  • Record the return/reset status and completion date.
  • Mark the laptop as Clean / Ready for Reassignment once the reset is complete.

⚠ Exceptions / Escalation

Stop the process and escalate before wiping if:

  • The device contains unsynced records subject to a grant, legal, personnel, accounting, records-retention, or investigation requirement
  • The drive is failing
  • The reset cannot complete
  • The device will leave CLERC ownership
