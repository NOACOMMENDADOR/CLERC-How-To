Purpose

This SOP documents how CLERC uses Microsoft Intune compliance policies to monitor the security posture of company workstations. Compliance policies define the minimum security requirements each device should meet.

At CLERC, compliance is used for monitoring only. Non-compliant devices are flagged for admin review but are not blocked from accessing company resources.

Microsoft Intune Windows 11 Microsoft Defender BitLocker

What Compliance Policies Do

A compliance policy is a set of rules that Intune checks against each enrolled device on a recurring basis. Each rule evaluates a specific security feature (such as whether encryption is enabled or antivirus is running).

Devices that meet all rules are marked Compliant. Devices that fail one or more rules are marked Not Compliant.

ⓘ At CLERC, compliance is monitoring only. No devices are blocked or restricted based on compliance status. The compliance dashboard gives you visibility into which machines need attention, and you address issues on your own schedule.

CLERC Workstation Baseline Policy

The following requirements are enforced by the CLERC Workstation Baseline compliance policy:

Requirement Status What It Does
BitLocker encryption Required Encrypts the hard drive so data on a lost or stolen laptop cannot be read without the recovery key.
Firewall Required Windows Firewall must be enabled.
Antivirus Required An antivirus solution must be active on the device.
Antispyware Required An antispyware solution must be active.
Defender Antimalware Required Defender must be the active antimalware provider.
Real-time protection Required Defender's real-time scanning must be turned on.

Actions for noncompliance: Devices that fail a check are marked non-compliant after a 1-day grace period. No access is blocked.

⚠ If a third-party antivirus (Norton, McAfee, etc.) has been installed and replaced Defender as the active provider, the device will show as non-compliant for the Defender requirement. The fix is to uninstall the third-party antivirus. Defender reactivates automatically.

Section 1 Creating a New Compliance Policy

Use these steps if you need to create an additional compliance policy or replace the existing one.

Step 1

Sign in to the Intune portal

Open intune.microsoft.com and sign in with your CLERC admin account.

Step 2

Navigate to compliance policies

Devices ➜ Compliance ➜ Policies ➜ Create policy
Step 3

Select the platform

For Platform, select Windows 10 and later. Click Create.

Step 4

Name the policy

Enter a name (e.g., CLERC Workstation Baseline) and an optional description.

Step 5

Configure compliance settings

On the Compliance settings page:

Under Device Security

Set the following to Require:

  • BitLocker
  • Firewall
  • Antivirus
  • Antispyware
  • Defender Antimalware
  • Real-time protection

Leave all other settings at Not configured.

Step 6

Set actions for noncompliance

On the Actions for noncompliance page:

  • Set Mark device noncompliant to 1 day (gives devices a 24-hour grace period before being flagged).
  • Leave other actions at their defaults.
Step 7

Assign the policy

On the Assignments page, add your device group (e.g., CLERC Workstations) under Included groups.

Step 8

Review and create

Review the settings and click Create.

ⓘ The Default Device Compliance Policy that appears on every device is a built-in Intune policy. You did not create it and cannot delete it. It marks all devices as compliant unless you change the tenant-wide setting under Devices > Compliance > Compliance policy settings.

Section 2 Editing an Existing Compliance Policy

Step 1

Open the policy list

Devices ➜ Compliance ➜ Policies
Step 2

Select the policy

Click on the policy you want to change.

Step 3

Open properties

Click Properties.

Step 4

Edit the relevant section

Click Edit next to the section you want to change (Compliance settings, Actions, or Assignments).

Step 5

Save changes

Make your changes and click Save.

⚠ Changes take effect on the next device sync cycle (up to 8 hours). To apply immediately, force a sync manually (see Section 4).

Section 3 Checking Device Compliance Status

View All Devices

Devices ➜ All devices

The Compliance column shows each device's status: Compliant, Not compliant, or Not evaluated.

View a Single Device

  1. Click on any device, then click Device compliance in the left sidebar.
  2. Each policy assigned to the device is listed with its status.
  3. Click on a policy to see which specific settings passed or failed.
ⓘ A device showing Not evaluated means it has not yet synced since the policy was assigned. Force a sync (Section 4) and check again after a few minutes.

Section 4 Forcing a Device Sync

Devices sync with Intune approximately every 8 hours. To force an immediate sync:

From the Device

  1. On the device, go to Settings > Accounts > Access work or school.
  2. Click on the work account.
  3. Click Info, then click Sync.

The device will check in with Intune and re-evaluate all assigned policies. Allow a few minutes for the status to update in the Intune portal.

From Intune (Remote)

You can also trigger a sync remotely from the Intune portal:

Devices ➜ All devices ➜ [select device] ➜ Sync (top toolbar)

Section 5 Troubleshooting Non-Compliant Devices

🔒 BitLocker Shows Not Compliant

Device is not encrypted: BitLocker may not have been enabled. Open an admin Command Prompt on the device and run:

manage-bde -status C:

If the volume is not encrypted, enable BitLocker through Settings > Privacy & security > Device encryption.

Recovery key not escrowed: If the device was encrypted before enrollment, the key may not have been backed up to Intune. Run the following in an admin PowerShell on the device:

$BLV = Get-BitLockerVolume -MountPoint "C:"
BackupToAAD-BitLockerKeyProtector -MountPoint "C:" -KeyProtectorId $BLV.KeyProtector[1].KeyProtectorId

Windows Home edition: BitLocker is not available on Windows Home. The device must be running Windows Pro or Enterprise.

🛡 Defender / Antivirus Shows Not Compliant

Third-party antivirus installed: If Norton, McAfee, or another antivirus has been installed, it replaces Defender as the active provider. Uninstall the third-party antivirus through Settings > Apps. Defender will reactivate automatically.

Real-time protection disabled: Open Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Manage settings and verify that Real-time protection is turned on.

🛡 Firewall Shows Not Compliant

Open Windows Security > Firewall & network protection. Ensure the firewall is turned on for all three network profiles: Domain, Private, and Public.

⚠️ Device Shows as Personal (Limited Compliance)

Devices enrolled as Personal (instead of Work or School) during initial setup have limited Intune management. Compliance policies may not fully apply, and BitLocker key escrow will not work.

The fix is to reset the device and re-enroll it as corporate:

Settings ➜ System ➜ Recovery ➜ Reset this PC ➜ Remove everything

During the fresh setup, select Set up for work or school and sign in with the employee's CLERC Microsoft account.

⚠ Back up any files the employee needs before resetting the device.

Section 6 Accessing BitLocker Recovery Keys

If an employee is locked out of their device and prompted for a BitLocker recovery key:

Step 1

Sign in to the Intune portal

Open intune.microsoft.com and sign in with your admin account.

Step 2

Navigate to the device

Devices ➜ All devices ➜ [select the locked device]
Step 3

Open recovery keys

Click Recovery keys in the left sidebar.

Step 4

Provide the key to the employee

Read the recovery key to the employee so they can enter it on their device.

⚠ If no recovery key appears, the key was not escrowed to Intune. This happens when a device was encrypted before enrollment or was enrolled as a Personal device. See the troubleshooting section above for how to fix this going forward.
